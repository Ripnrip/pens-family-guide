Florida's 2026 school seizure law

Florida CS/HB 1201 took effect July 1, 2026. A student's Individualized Seizure Action Plan (ISAP) must be developed and signed by a medical professional, charter schools are included, and school staff who teach or transport the student need seizure training.

A summary for families, not legal advice. Check your school district's policy for details.

Ready for hurricane season

Atlantic hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30. For kids on seizure medicines, plan ahead: keep extra medicine on hand, pack a copy of the seizure action plan and a medicine list in your go-bag, and talk with your pharmacist about refills before a storm.

Safety & privacy

Careful by design

No patient information collected

PENS in ChatGPT and Claude never asks for names, birthdays or medical records. Please don't share them in chat; call the office about your child's care.

General information, not medical advice

Answers are general education. They don't replace a visit with your child's care team or their own care plan.

Emergencies: call 911

If a seizure lasts 5 minutes or longer, or your child has trouble breathing, call 911 right away. Don't wait for a chat reply.

Reviewed by PENS clinicians

Every clinical answer is reviewed by PENS clinicians before launch, so it reflects how the PENS team cares for kids.

In an emergency, call 911. For questions about your child's care, call PENS at (813) 873-7367.

For pediatricians & schools

Help for the people around the child

Pediatricians

Referring a patient to PENS

Ask PENS what to include and get a ready-to-use referral checklist:

  • Referral order with reason and diagnosis code
  • Recent office notes, plus EEG and MRI/CT reports
  • Current medicines and allergies
  • Insurance details and parent contact information

Send referrals by fax at (813) 875-9722 or by phone, never through chat. Emergencies belong in the emergency department.

Schools

Getting seizure-ready at school

School nurses, teachers and families can use the Seizure-Ready Circle to understand:

  • What an Individualized Seizure Action Plan (ISAP) is
  • Who should have a copy: teachers, bus drivers, coaches
  • How families request a signed plan from PENS
  • Where to find seizure training for staff

Free staff training is available from the Epilepsy Foundation.
